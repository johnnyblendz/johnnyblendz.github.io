About The Shop

Trained hands, no rush jobs.

We opened Johnny Blendz because every fast cut we got left something to be desired. This shop is built around the fundamentals: sharp tools, real technique, and enough time to actually finish the job right.

Every barber on our floor apprenticed for years before touching a paying customer's head. We still believe in that standard.
